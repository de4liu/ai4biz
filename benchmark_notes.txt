COURSE 1: Wharton AI for Business Specialization (Coursera)
Institution: University of Pennsylvania / Wharton
Format: 4-course self-paced specialization | ~16 weeks total | online
Audience: Non-technical business professionals; explicitly labeled "beginner"
Source: https://www.coursera.org/specializations/ai-for-business-wharton
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WEEK/TOPIC STRUCTURE (4 courses x 4 weeks each):

Course 1 – AI Fundamentals for Non-Data Scientists
  - Wk 1: Big Data basics; 3 types of machine learning (supervised/unsupervised/reinforcement)
  - Wk 2: Algorithm training; neural networks; deep learning
  - Wk 3: GANs, VAEs; data's role in ML systems
  - Wk 4: Industry interviews; real-world use cases

Course 2 – AI Applications in Marketing and Finance
  - Wk 1: Customer journeys; AI behavioral influence
  - Wk 2: Personalization and recommendation algorithms
  - Wk 3-4: Credit risk assessment; fraud detection (supervised + unsupervised ML)

Course 3 – AI Applications in People Management
  - Wk 1: HR decision-making processes
  - Wk 2: Data types; predictive analytics for talent
  - Wk 3: Hiring bias; algorithm limitations
  - Wk 4: Algorithmic bias mitigation; blockchain applications

Course 4 – AI Strategy and Governance
  - Wk 1: AutoML tools; economic impacts of AI
  - Wk 2: Big Data implementation strategies
  - Wk 3: Algorithmic fairness; explainable AI
  - Wk 4: Ethical frameworks; legal compliance

TOOLS USED:
  - Teachable Machine (hands-on ML model building, no code)
  - Explainable AI frameworks (conceptual)
  - TensorFlow referenced conceptually

ASSESSMENT TYPES:
  - Weekly video lectures + readings (~2 hrs combined/week)
  - 1 assignment per week
  - Peer-reviewed assignments
  - Digital badge / certificate on completion of specialization

AI LITERACY FOR NON-TECHNICAL STUDENTS:
  - Explicitly beginner-level; no prerequisites
  - Instructors explain technical concepts without requiring math/coding background
  - Real-world business examples anchor every module
  - No coding required; Teachable Machine provides tactile ML exposure without code

HYBRID EXAM + PROJECT NOTES:
  - Assessment is entirely assignment-based (no formal exams)
  - Peer review substitutes for instructor grading on projects
  - No capstone project identified

FRAMEWORK: AACSB — AI in Business Education (January 2026)
Source: https://www.aacsb.edu/insights/reports/2026/a-framework-for-artificial-intelligence-in-business-education
Scope: Evidence from 48 business schools; captures shift from experimentation to implementation

8 CONVERGENT THEMES:
  1. Comprehensive ecosystems — infrastructure for coordination + sustainability
  2. Democratized AI education — AI literacy across ALL learning pathways, not specialist tracks only
  3. Domain-specific applications — AI embedded in discipline-specific courses (finance, marketing, ops)
  4. Faculty development — identified as THE critical success factor
  5. Responsible AI and ethics — integrated throughout curricula (not standalone)
  6. Strategic partnerships — industry + peer institution collaboration
  7. Pedagogical innovation — transforming HOW learning happens
  8. Leadership in transformation — balancing urgency with institutional values

IMPLICATIONS FOR NON-TECHNICAL CURRICULUM:
  - AI literacy = foundational competency, not specialized elective
  - Ethics embedded in every module; never siloed at end
  - Domain application skills > generic AI knowledge
  - Assessment should reflect application (projects) not recall (exams)
  - Faculty capability is the bottleneck — design curriculum faculty can teach
